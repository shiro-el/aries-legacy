Herd: three layouts

Gold appears only when an agent needs you. Filled star: blocked, waiting on you. Outlined star: done, ready to review. Solid dot: working. Hollow dot: idle. Dashed dot: unknown. Everything else stays in charcoal and fleece.

A. Constellation

Grouped by workspace and tab. Agents in one tab hang on one line, like the stars on the ram's flank, so you see which agents belong to the same piece of work.

B. Needs you, then the sky

Agents that need you come first as cards you can act on without leaving the screen. Below them, every tab collapses to one line with its agents drawn as a small constellation.

C. Roster with a reply bar

A flat list in the order the queue already sorts by. The top agent that needs you gets a reply bar with the >- prompt from the logo, so a quick answer takes one tap.
